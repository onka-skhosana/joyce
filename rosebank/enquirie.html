Rosebank College Enquiries

Dear Rosebank International Student, A warm welcome to Rosebank International. We are delighted to have you join our dynamic academic community of over 35,000 students across eight campuses and our online learning platforms as you begin this exciting academic chapter in 2026.

At Rosebank International, your success is at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to creating an environment that supports academic excellence, personal growth, and career readiness. As a student of Rosebank International, you are part of an institution that values innovation, integrity, and inclusivity, ensuring a learning experience that is both enriching and rewarding.

The Finance Office will assist with tuition fees, student accounts, and payment arrangements queries. Regular Community Updates: Stay connected with campus news, achievements, and upcoming events through the student newsletter. A key highlight for 2025 is the expansion of our Online Student Support Centres in Braamfontein, Cape Town, and Pretoria. These centres provide dedicated spaces for library research, quiet study, and computer labs, ensuring all students have access to essential academic resources. For efficient assistance with academic support, student services, or financial enquiries, please submit a request via RI Assist. We encourage you to take full advantage of the resources available to you and to actively engage with our academic and support teams. We look forward to supporting you throughout your academic journey and celebrating your future success.
